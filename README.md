Credit Card Launch Strategy & Customer Segmentation Analysis

### Objective:
Analyze customer data to identify target segments for launching a new credit card and provide business recommendations.

### Tools Used:
- SQL
- Excel
- Power BI

### Key Insights:
- Low-income customers contribute the highest share of total revenue, indicating a high-volume transaction-driven segment.
- Mid-income customers represent a high-growth opportunity and can be converted into high-value users through targeted incentives.
- Customers aged 26–40 show the highest transaction frequency, making them ideal for lifestyle-focused credit offerings.
- A segment of customers with high credit limits but low activity indicates inefficient credit allocation and potential financial risk.
- High transaction frequency does not always translate to high transaction value, highlighting the need for differentiated reward strategies.

### Business Recommendations:
- Launch premium credit cards targeting high-spending customers with benefits like cash-back, travel rewards, and higher limits.
- Introduce mid-tier cards with EMI options, cash-back incentives, and milestone rewards to convert mid-income customers.
- Offer low-limit, low-risk cards for low-income customers to ensure financial inclusion while minimizing default risk.
- Optimize credit limits for underutilized accounts to improve credit efficiency and reduce exposure.
- Implement targeted marketing strategies based on customer segmentation to improve engagement and card adoption.
